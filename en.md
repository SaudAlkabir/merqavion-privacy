# Merqavion — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 21 November 2026
**Publisher:** Saud-Alkabir Ba Alawi
**Privacy and support email:** merqavion.support@gmail.com
**Country:** United Arab Emirates

## 1. Introduction

Merqavion ("Business Management Suite") is a Windows desktop application for managing a
business's products, suppliers, purchasing, inventory, sales, invoices and finances. It is
published by Saud-Alkabir Ba Alawi.

This policy explains what information Merqavion processes, where it is kept, when the app uses
the internet, and how you can export or delete your information.

Merqavion does not require an online account. Your business records are stored on your own
computer and are not sent to the publisher.

## 2. Information Merqavion processes

Depending on what you enter, Merqavion may process:

- Your company details (names in English and Arabic, address, tax number) that appear on invoices.
- Customer and supplier names, contact names and roles, telephone numbers, email addresses,
  addresses, tax identifiers and notes.
- Products, prices, quotations, purchase orders, shipments, import costs and stock movements.
- Sales documents, invoices, credit notes, payments, receipts and expenses.
- Supplier evaluations and ratings, tasks and reminders.
- Documents and images you attach, with their original file name, size, type, a checksum (SHA-256)
  and the time they were added. Files and free-text fields can contain any information you put in them.
- App preferences such as language and theme.

You decide what information you enter. Merqavion copies company and customer details into
issued invoices and credit notes so these documents keep the details they were issued with.

## 3. How the information is used

The information is used only to provide the app's features: keeping your records, calculating
totals, taxes, stock values and balances, showing reports and reminders, printing and exporting
documents, and creating backups.

## 4. Where your data is stored

- Merqavion stores its data **locally on your Windows device**, in a folder for your Windows user
  account. The default folder is `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CompanyManagement`. When the app is installed
  from the Microsoft Store, Windows may keep this folder inside the app's private package data.
- The data consists of a SQLite database, a folder of attached files and a small settings file.
- When the database format is upgraded, Merqavion first saves a copy of the previous database in
  the data folder. When you restore a backup, the data that was replaced is kept in the data folder.
- **Merqavion does not encrypt** the database, attached files, settings or backups. Protect your
  device and Windows account, for example with device encryption (BitLocker or Device Encryption),
  a password and secure storage for your backups.

## 5. Internet use: Microsoft Store purchases and licences

Merqavion is free to use. **Merqavion Pro** is an optional one-time in-app purchase sold through
the Microsoft Store.

- When you open **Settings > Merqavion Pro**, choose **Upgrade to Pro** or **Restore purchases**,
  or start the app, Merqavion asks Windows and the Microsoft Store for the Pro product, its price
  and whether your Microsoft account owns it. These requests are made by Windows and the
  Microsoft Store and can use the internet. They are handled by Microsoft under the
  [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) and involve your
  Microsoft account and device as Microsoft describes there.
- Payment takes place in the Microsoft Store's own purchase window. **Merqavion never receives or
  stores your payment card or billing details.** The publisher receives sales reports from
  Microsoft as Microsoft provides them to app publishers.
- Merqavion does not send your business records in these requests and does not store its own
  "purchased" flag; Windows keeps the Store licence.

Apart from these Microsoft Store requests, Merqavion has no cloud sync, no telemetry or analytics,
no advertising, no automatic crash reports and no remote access by the publisher. All business
features work offline.

Windows, the Microsoft Store, printers, the programs that open your files, and any cloud-synced
folders you choose have their own behaviour and privacy terms.

## 6. Files, attachments and exports

- Files you attach are copied into Merqavion's data folder.
- When you open an attachment, Merqavion places a temporary copy in your Windows temporary folder
  and opens it with the program Windows uses for that file type. **These temporary copies are not
  removed automatically**; you can delete them from your temporary folder.
- Printing and "Save as PDF" use Windows printing (for example Microsoft Print to PDF) and
  temporary print files that the app tries to remove.
- CSV exports, PDFs, saved attachments and backups are saved where you choose. They are your
  files; Merqavion does not manage or delete them.

## 7. Backups

Settings > Backup & data creates a ZIP file with your database, attached files and settings in a
location you choose. Backups contain all your business and personal information and are **not
encrypted**. Keep them somewhere safe. Restoring a backup replaces the current data on the device.

## 8. Keeping and deleting your data

- Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete or archive records in the
  app; some records can only be archived because other documents refer to them. Archiving keeps
  the information.
- There is no single "erase everything" command. To remove all data, close Merqavion and delete
  its data folder, any temporary attachment copies, and the backups and exports you saved elsewhere.
- **Uninstalling:** uninstalling or resetting the app can remove its data folder, including the
  database and attachments. Create a backup in a separate location before you uninstall. Backups
  and exports you saved outside the app's data folder are not removed by uninstalling.
- Deleting files normally does not securely erase them from the storage device.
- Buying or restoring Merqavion Pro does not change or delete business data. Restoring your Pro
  purchase on another device does not copy your data; use a backup to move your records.

## 9. Sharing

Merqavion does not share your information with the publisher or anyone else. You may choose to
share exported files, invoices, documents or backups; the people and services you share them
with handle them under their own terms.

If you email the publisher, the publisher receives your email address and whatever you include in
your message. Please do not send backups or business records unless they are needed to answer your
question.

## 10. Security

Merqavion checks the integrity of attachments and backups and validates its data, but no
software can guarantee complete security. Because the app does not encrypt its files, the
security of your data depends on the security of your device, Windows account and backup locations.

## 11. Children

Merqavion is a business application and is not directed to children.

## 12. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when Merqavion's features or data handling change. The current
version is published at the privacy-policy address shown in the Microsoft Store listing.
